How to Cancel Life Time Fitness

Difficult — CFS 4/5
Method: in_person · Last verified: 2026-08-24 · Official site

Cancellation Steps

  1. Prepare written cancellation notice — Life Time does not accept phone-only cancellations for full membership termination.
  2. Submit the notice in person at your home club, through certified mail, or via the Life Time app/website account settings, depending on your club's current policy. Policy on which channels are accepted has varied by club and by year; confirm current options directly with your club before relying on a specific method.
  3. Provide the required 30 days' advance notice. Your membership and billing continue through that full 30-day window regardless of when you stop attending.
  4. Get a dated, written confirmation of your cancellation request and keep it. Complaints describe disputes over whether notice was properly received or processed in time.

⚠ Watch Out — Dark Patterns

⚠ forced continuity

Members describe a 30-day notice requirement that functions as a mandatory extra billing cycle regardless of actual usage, with multiple complaint-board reports of billing continuing 5+ weeks past a submitted cancellation request.

⚠ reinstatement rate loss

BBB complaints document members losing a legacy or discounted membership rate permanently after cancelling, even when the member intended to return, with no advance disclosure that canceling forfeits the rate.

What to Expect (Retention Tactics)

If you contact support, they will likely offer:

  • 30-day mandatory notice period applied uniformly regardless of how a member submits cancellation.
  • No universally available simple online self-cancel button; some members report being told they must visit in person.
  • Reinstated memberships are not guaranteed the same rate as the original membership.

You do not need to accept any of these. Say: "I would like to cancel my account completely." Repeat as needed. You do not need to provide a reason.
